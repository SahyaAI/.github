# SahyaLabs

## Confidential repositories

SahyaLabs develops **VPAS**, a pilot assist system for the Sahya CX1 air taxi, and **SahyaAI**, a platform that improves it across fleets. Our repositories are private and confidential.

To view or clone them, you need to sign an agreement first:

| You want to | Agreement | Read and sign |
|---|---|---|
| Review or evaluate our work | **SAHYA-NDA-R** v1.1: Non-Disclosure Agreement for reviewers and visitors | [SAHYA-NDA-R_v1.1.pdf](https://github.com/SahyaAI/.github/blob/main/agreements/SAHYA-NDA-R_v1.1.pdf) |
| Contribute code, designs or documents | **SAHYA-NDA-C** v1.1: Non-Disclosure and IP Assignment Agreement for contributors | [SAHYA-NDA-C_v1.1.pdf](https://github.com/SahyaAI/.github/blob/main/agreements/SAHYA-NDA-C_v1.1.pdf) |

## How it works

1. **Read** the agreement: download the PDF above.
2. **Sign** it: fill in the signature page (full name, email, organisation, GitHub username; for contributors your Background IP, or "none"), sign in the box with [Adobe Acrobat Fill & Sign](https://www.adobe.com/acrobat/online/sign-pdf.html) (free) or any PDF application that can sign, and email the signed PDF to info@sahyalabs.com from the email address you entered. If you decide not to sign, nothing happens and you get no access.
3. **Get access.** We confirm your email address, countersign, and send you the fully signed agreement. You then receive a GitHub invitation to the matching team. Accept it while signed in as the GitHub user you entered.
4. **Keep it safe.** Use two-factor authentication on GitHub, do not fork or re-host the repositories, and delete your copies when the agreement ends.

Questions: info@sahyalabs.com.
